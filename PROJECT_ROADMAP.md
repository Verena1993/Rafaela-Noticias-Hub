Version: 0.3

Status: Active Development

Last Updated: June 2026

# Vision

RN Hub is being developed as a professional newsroom management platform.

The objective is to centralize the entire editorial workflow, from the arrival of a news tip to its publication and subsequent analysis.

The project prioritizes:

* scalability;
* maintainability;
* performance;
* consistency;
* user experience.
